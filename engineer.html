Engineer: Phase 3 of DEEDS

Where ideas become real.

Students take on a professional role to design, plan, build, and refine a solution that directly addresses a root cause of the challenge, and demonstrate that it is ready for real-world use.

By the End

Success Criteria

By the end of Engineer, students should demonstrate growth across three core areas of design and professional construction:

1

Design the Solution

Make clear design choices that address a specific root cause, meet defined criteria and constraints, and can be justified with evidence and reasoning.

NGSS ETS1-1, ETS1-2 · CCSS W.2, MP.4 · C3 D4.7
2

Plan & Build

Develop a logical construction sequence, use professional tools and disciplinary methods safely and effectively, and follow the plan while adapting as needed.

NGSS SEP 3, ETS1-3 · CCSS W.4 · C3 D4
3

Test, Revise & Demonstrate Readiness

Test the solution during construction, revise based on feedback and evidence, and demonstrate that it functions as intended and is ready for enactment.

NGSS ETS1-3 · CCSS W.5, SL.4 · C3 D4.6
See How It Works

Student Experience

~8–12 sessions

Engineer mirrors a real professional design-build process: students define clear requirements, plan carefully, and construct and iterate their solution.

Step 1
Set Criteria & Constraints

Define what the solution must do and what limits it must work within.

Step 2
Plan the Build

Develop a roadmap for creating your solution, including the roles, materials, and tools you will use.

Step 3
Build & Iterate

Construct, test, and refine the solution using professional tools, iterating based on evidence and feedback.

Step 4
Demonstrate Readiness

Show that the solution works as intended and is ready for real-world use.

Grounded in the Science of Learning and Development

In Engineer, students build high-quality feedback, self-efficacy, and sense of control as they turn their thinking into a real solution.

They test prototypes and gather feedback, creating natural loops for improvement. Working through a scaffolded progression of challenging but achievable goals, they see the connection between effort and success, especially as they revise and improve through iteration. They also exercise agency, making authentic decisions about how to design and construct their solution.
