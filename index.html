About DEEDS

What is DEEDS?

DEEDS is a hands-on learning model built around real-world problems. Students take on the role of problem solvers and changemakers. They connect with their communities, discover significant issues, and design and share real solutions. Along the way, they build core content knowledge and personal and professional skills.

The Five Phases

Every DEEDS unit follows a repeatable five-phase process that students can apply to any challenge. Each phase includes core protocols that advance content learning and build the habits of mind students need to create meaningful change.

Phase 01 Discover Students see, hear, and feel the challenge before they study it, building empathy and a personal "why." Phase 02 Examine Students investigate the challenge deeply using disciplinary content, skills, and evidence. Phase 03 Engineer Students design, prototype, and refine a response to the challenge. Phase 04 Do Students implement their solution and take action in the community. Phase 05 Share Students present their work publicly and reflect on what they've learned and contributed.
