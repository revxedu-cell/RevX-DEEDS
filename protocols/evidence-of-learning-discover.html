DISCOVER Evidence of Learning
Evidence of Learning
Scholars are ready to move on from Discover when they can demonstrate the following:
Success Criteria
1
Frame and Define the Challenge
Ask questions and use evidence to name a real-world problem worth investigating.
2
Understand Human Impact
Examine different perspectives to understand who is affected and how.
3
Articulate Purpose & Significance
Make evidence-based claims about why the problem is important and what should change.
Scholars should be able to respond thoroughly and thoughtfully to the following prompts:
1
What is the problem we are learning about? Use something from Discover to explain it.
2
Who is affected? Name two people or groups and tell how it affects them.
3
Why is this problem important? Tell why it matters and what you think should change.
1
What challenge are we investigating? Include at least one piece of evidence from Discover.
2
Who is affected and how? Identify at least two stakeholders and explain the impact on each.
3
Why does this problem matter? Explain its importance and what you think should change.
1
What is the challenge? Define the problem with at least one piece of evidence from Discover.
2

3

What is the challenge? Define the problem and support your explanation with evidence from Discover.
