ENGINEER Evidence of Learning
Evidence of Learning
Scholars are ready to move on from Engineer when they can demonstrate the following:
Success Criteria
1
Design the Solution
Make clear design choices that address a specific root cause, meet defined criteria and constraints, and can be justified with evidence and reasoning.
2
Plan & Build
Develop a logical construction sequence, use professional tools and disciplinary methods safely and effectively, and follow the plan while adapting as needed.
3
Test, Revise & Demonstrate Readiness
Test the solution during construction, revise based on feedback and evidence, and demonstrate that it functions as intended and is ready for enactment.
Scholars should be able to respond thoroughly and thoughtfully to the following prompts:
1
Does your solution work? Show how it does what it needs to do.
2
Is your solution ready to use? What would you still want to fix or improve?
3
How does your solution help with the problem we were solving?
1
Show how your solution works. Does it meet your criteria? What would you fix if you had more time?
2
What changes did you make while building? Why did you make them?
3
How well does your solution address the challenge? What is it missing?
1
Demonstrate how your solution works and explain how it meets your defined criteria and constraints.
2
What did you change during construction and why? What does your Revision Log show?
3
How ready is your solution for real-world use? What limitations remain?
1
Demonstrate readiness: explain how your solution addresses the root cause, how it meets defined criteria and constraints, and how your revisions improved it.
2
What tradeoffs did you make during the build, and how do you justify them?
3
What would need to happen for this solution to have real impact? What are its limits?
