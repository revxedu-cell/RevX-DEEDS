Evidence of Learning
Scholars are ready to move on from Share when they can demonstrate the following:
Success Criteria
1
Communicate Learning
Explain what was learned and done clearly to an audience outside the classroom, referencing evidence from across the full project to support explanations.
2
Reflect on Growth
Reflect on how thinking, skills, and sense of self developed across the project — using specific examples to explain what changed and why it matters.
3
Engage with Your Audience
Respond thoughtfully to questions, critique, or feedback — clarifying, defending, or building on ideas when challenged by audience members.
Scholars should be able to respond thoroughly and thoughtfully to the following prompts:
1
What did you share with your audience? What did they say?
2
How have you grown as a learner during this project?
3
What do you want to do next to keep helping with this challenge?
1
What did you explain to your audience, and what did they think?
2
How have you grown as a learner and teammate? Give one specific example.
3
What would you do differently — in your project work or in your presentation?
1
How well did you communicate your learning? What were you strongest at, and what would you improve?
2
How have you grown during this project — in your thinking, your skills, or your sense of what you can do?
3
What questions from your audience challenged your thinking, and how did you respond?
1
Reflect on your presentation: how effectively did you communicate your learning, support your claims with evidence, and engage your audience?
2
How have you grown as a thinker, collaborator, and changemaker across this project? Use specific examples.
3
What do you now believe about the challenge and your solution that you didn’t believe at the start — and what does that tell you about what to do next?
